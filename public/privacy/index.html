PRIVACY POLICY · EFFECTIVE 1 NOVEMBER 2026

Your images stay yours.

This policy describes how the PixelTools website handles information when you visit the site or use its currently available image tools. It is written for the Pakistan-focused release and should be read with the limitations below.

1. Who operates PixelTools

PixelTools is a web-based image utility project published at pixeltools-9xe.pages.dev and maintained through its public GitHub repository. This policy refers to the project as “PixelTools”, “we” or “us”. The repository does not currently publish a registered company name, postal address or dedicated privacy email. Do not send sensitive personal information through public issue trackers.

2. Image files and browser-local processing

The 14 currently available image tools process the image you select inside your browser using browser APIs. The selected image is not uploaded to a PixelTools-operated image-processing server. Image data and temporary previews may remain in your browser's memory while a tool is open. Closing the tool or page ends the page's use of that in-memory working data, subject to normal browser behavior.

When you choose to download an output, your browser saves it to the location configured on your device. PixelTools does not receive that downloaded file. Your browser, operating system, extensions, backups or other software may separately retain files according to their own settings.

The Metadata Viewer reads available image metadata, which may include camera details, dates or GPS coordinates, locally in your browser. Treat metadata and Base64 output as potentially sensitive. The Metadata Cleaner re-encodes an image rather than copying the source container; browser encoders may add generic output metadata, so it is not a forensic sanitization guarantee.

3. Website and technical information

PixelTools does not currently require an account and does not ask you to submit personal information to use the image tools. The current release does not provide an image-upload API, payment checkout, advertising profile or first-party analytics account.

To deliver the website and protect it against abuse, the hosting and network provider (Cloudflare Pages / Cloudflare) may process technical request and security information such as IP address, request time, requested URL, browser or device details and security events. This processing is performed by the provider under its own terms, privacy documentation and service configuration. PixelTools does not control the provider's independent infrastructure logs or retention periods. Review Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

4. Fonts, cookies and third-party services

The website uses system fonts and does not currently request web fonts from Google Fonts or another remote font service. PixelTools does not currently set advertising or analytics cookies as part of its application.

The hosting and network provider may use technical mechanisms necessary for delivery, security or operation. Their behavior is governed by the provider's service configuration and privacy documentation. This statement is not a guarantee that no third-party technical processing or browser storage occurs.

5. Purpose, sharing and retention

PixelTools uses information available to the project to operate, maintain, secure and improve the website, respond to support requests you choose to submit, and address abuse or legal requirements. We do not sell image files or personal information. We do not receive the image files processed by the current browser-local tools.

Information you post in a public GitHub issue or discussion is handled by GitHub and may be publicly visible. Do not post private images, passwords, identity documents, GPS data or other sensitive information there. Technical logs held by hosting providers are subject to those providers' retention and deletion practices.

6. Your choices and requests

  • You can choose not to use the website or not to select a file.
  • You can close a tool or page to stop its current processing workflow.
  • You can manage downloaded files, browser permissions, extensions and browser data through your device and browser settings.
  • For a general question, use the Contact page. Do not include sensitive personal data in a public issue. A private privacy-request channel is not currently published, so avoid sending confidential material until a private contact route is provided.

7. Children

PixelTools is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to the project, do not post the details publicly; use the available project contact route without including the information itself.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures in the website code and hosting configuration to reduce security risks, but no website, browser, device or network can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your browser and device updated and avoid selecting images you are not authorized to process. Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately through GitHub's security advisory reporting route when private reporting is enabled; do not publish exploit details or user data.

9. Pakistan and applicable law

PixelTools is operated as a Pakistan-focused project. Applicable Pakistani laws may govern relevant activities, including the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016, as amended, and other laws that apply to the circumstances. This policy does not claim that a comprehensive personal-data protection statute or a particular statutory privacy-rights regime is in force. The status of proposed legislation may change; nothing here limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

10. Changes and contact

We may revise this policy when the website, its providers or applicable law changes. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. For non-sensitive questions, use the Contact page. Do not put confidential information in a public GitHub issue.
