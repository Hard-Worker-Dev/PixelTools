How to use Image Blur

Upload an image, adjust blur strength, choose an output format and download the result.

Images are processed in your browser for this tool. PixelTools does not upload your selected image to its own image-processing server.

Frequently asked questions

Can I adjust blur strength?

Yes. The blur slider lets you choose a strength from subtle to stronger blur.

Is the source file changed?

No. PixelTools creates a separate output file.
