PDF Rotator

Rotate every page in a PDF by 90, 180 or 270 degrees clockwise, or 90 degrees counterclockwise. Free local browser processing.

Open PDF Rotator →

How to use PDF Rotator

Choose a PDF up to 25 MB and 200 pages, select the rotation direction, then export the rotated document. Existing page rotations are adjusted rather than discarded. Processing happens locally in your browser; password-protected PDFs are not supported.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate all pages at once?

Yes. The PDF Rotator applies the selected quarter-turn to every page in the document.

What rotation angles are available?

Choose 90°, 180° or 270° clockwise, or 90° counterclockwise.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The PDF is processed locally in your browser.
