How to use Rounded Corners

Upload an image, adjust the corner radius, process it and download the transparent PNG result.

Images are processed in your browser for this tool. PixelTools does not upload your selected image to its own image-processing server.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the output PNG?

PNG preserves transparency around the rounded corners. JPG cannot preserve transparent pixels.

Can I control the radius?

Yes. Use the radius slider to adjust the corner roundness.
