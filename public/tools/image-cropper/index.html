How to use Image Cropper

Upload an image, enter crop position and dimensions in pixels, choose an output format and download the crop.

Images are processed in your browser for this tool. PixelTools does not upload your selected image to its own image-processing server.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if the crop crosses an edge?

The crop area is safely constrained to the source image boundaries.

Does cropping reduce quality?

The crop is rendered into a new image using the selected browser-supported output format and quality setting.
