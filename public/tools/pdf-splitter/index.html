Open PDF Splitter →

How to split PDF pages

Choose one PDF and enter comma-separated page numbers or ranges. For example, 1-3, 5, 8-10 creates three separate PDFs: pages 1–3, page 5 and pages 8–10. The ZIP download contains one PDF for each range.

The source PDF is limited to 25 MB and 200 pages. The combined output and ZIP archive are limited to 150 MB. Overlapping, reversed and out-of-range selections are rejected. Password-protected or damaged PDFs are not supported. Processing takes place locally in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I select pages?

Enter comma-separated page numbers or ranges, such as 1-3, 5, 8-10. Each range becomes a separate PDF.

What is the page limit?

The source PDF can be up to 25 MB and 200 pages. Split output is limited to 150 MB.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

No. PDF files are processed locally in your browser. Password-protected PDFs are not supported.
