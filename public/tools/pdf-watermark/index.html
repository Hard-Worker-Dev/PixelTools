PDF Watermark

Add a text watermark to every page of a PDF. Adjust color, opacity, font size, rotation and placement, with processing performed locally in your browser.

Open PDF Watermark →

How to use PDF Watermark

Select a PDF up to 25 MB and 200 pages. Enter up to 120 characters using basic English letters, numbers and punctuation. Choose a color, 5–100% opacity, 8–96 pt font size, -90° to 90° rotation and one of five positions. Download the watermarked PDF when processing completes. Output is limited to 150 MB.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the watermark appearance?

Yes. Choose its color, opacity, font size, rotation and one of five page positions.

Is the watermark added to every page?

Yes. The text watermark is applied to every page in the selected PDF.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. Processing runs locally in your browser. Password-protected PDFs are not supported.
