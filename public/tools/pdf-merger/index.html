PDF Merger

Combine multiple PDF files into one document. Arrange the source files in the order you want, merge them and download your finished PDF.

Open PDF Merger →

How to merge PDF files

Add 2 to 10 PDF documents, move files up or down to set their order, then choose Merge. Every page is copied into one new PDF in the displayed sequence.

Each source PDF is limited to 25 MB, combined input to 100 MB, the merged document to 200 pages and the final file to 150 MB. Password-protected or damaged PDFs are not supported. Processing takes place locally in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

How many PDFs can I merge?

Merge 2 to 10 PDF files, up to 25 MB each and 100 MB combined, with a maximum of 200 pages.

Can I change the page order?

Yes. Move files up or down before merging. Pages are combined in the order shown.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

No. PDF files are processed locally in your browser. Password-protected PDFs are not supported.
