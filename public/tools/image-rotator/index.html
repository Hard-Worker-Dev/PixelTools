How to use Image Rotator

Upload an image, rotate left or right in quarter-turn steps, choose an output format and process it.

Images are processed in your browser for this tool. PixelTools does not upload your selected image to its own image-processing server.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate by any angle?

The current rotator supports 90-degree increments to keep the output canvas predictable.

Does rotation upload my file?

No. Image rotation is performed locally in your browser.
